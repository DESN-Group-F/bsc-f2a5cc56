## Included behavior

All staff share working inventory and a separate demonstration inventory. Native accounts distinguish responsible owners, current holders and operation actors. Staff can register batteries and confirm their own checkouts or another holder's return; administrators maintain saved information, corrections and accounts.

The three status tabs remain available. One Filter panel contains the remaining criteria, with removable Applied filters above results. My batteries and My loans show personal responsibility and active borrowing over shared data. Filtered, current-page, selected and single-battery downloads support summaries or selectable complete details. Personal download scope is resolved from the authenticated account and rejects selected assets that have left that scope.

Optional manufacture and first-use dates support calculated age without inferring unknown dates. Storage is a building plus an optional room; observed location remains separate time/source evidence. J18 Willis Annexe is enabled. E10 Hilmer Building and G17 Electrical Engineering Building are disabled references. Demo room and Demo workspace are explicit J18 placeholders awaiting confirmation. Real battery records remain pending.

Versioned metadata, reviewed loan-state guards, atomic batches up to 100 batteries, safe retries and commit-time account checks support simultaneous staff operations. Migrations 0000 through 0007 define this source baseline.

## Verification and limits

The final implemented iteration passed 86 isolated tests and 70 local API checks, TypeScript, source ESLint and the production build. Browser checks verified personal counts, filtering, actual Excel downloads, location choices and a selection moving outside personal scope after another account's return. See [validation](../validation.md#unified-filters-personal-views-and-location-readiness) for actual results, corrected test assumptions and evidence locations.

This freeze publishes source, schema, fictional fixtures, tests and completed documentation. It excludes local account credentials, sessions, databases, generated builds, runtime dependencies, private QA artifacts and unfinished planning drafts. Required third-party licenses and functional hosting/authentication identifiers remain in their adapters.

No remote application deployment, real RFID ingestion, school identity integration, JAGGAER connection, automatic notification or safety agent is included. Departmental load capacity and stakeholder workload reduction have not been measured. Earlier frozen references remain unchanged.
