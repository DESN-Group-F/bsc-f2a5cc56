## Included behavior

All active staff share working inventory and a separate demonstration inventory, with individual accounts and server-enforced permissions. Staff can view/export all business data, register batteries and operate their own checkouts or another holder's returns. Administrators additionally manage saved metadata, responsible ownership, directories, accounts, charging evidence and reasoned history corrections. Owner, borrower, registered storage and dated observed location remain distinct.

The three battery status tabs, one Filter panel, removable Applied filters and Clear filters support shared and personal views. My batteries, My loans and My activity use native account identity. Downloads support filtered results, the current page, selected batteries across pages and single-battery details, with selectable complete histories. Manufacture and first-use dates support calculated age without guessing unknown dates. J18 is enabled; its rooms are explicit placeholders. E10 and G17 remain disabled reference buildings.

Persistent Scan checkout and Scan return stations support deliberate manual tag input and demonstration-only simulated reads. Continuous mode saves individual confirmed results; Batch mode requires confirmation and commits up to 100 batteries atomically. Verified successes clear their simulator selection, and only batteries eligible for the selected movement appear. A later borrowing round can be processed in the same station. Unknown tags, rejected states and uncertain writes remain visible; immutable captured requests and verified receipts prevent duplicate movements. Optional return placement records its time and source separately from registered storage.

Recurring tasks supplies three configurable provisional templates, native staff assignments and shared cycles. Messages is an individual inbox with unread indicators independent of To do/Completed task state. Assigned staff or administrators record completion; notes are optional. Plan creation and movement recovery retain the original request until resolved. Reviewed versions, exact loan bindings, atomic evidence and terminal request reservations guard contested operations and retries.

## Publication boundary and remaining work

The freeze contains application source, schema/migrations, fictional fixtures, tests and completed application documentation. Local credentials, sessions, business databases, generated builds, dependencies, private QA artifacts and independent research outside the implemented application are excluded and retained locally. Required third-party licenses and functional hosting/authentication identifiers remain in their adapters; they are not product attribution.

Real battery specifications, RFID hardware/output and verified room mapping remain pending. School SSO, JAGGAER, safety agents and external email are not connected. Reminders are evaluated during authenticated use; there is no unattended scheduler, and saved email preferences send no mail. Task templates are provisional and completion is not a safety certification. Catalog-assisted registration and physical inspection procedures are outside this application release.

Source publication does not deploy the application, reset the local inventory or change earlier frozen releases. Subsequent development must leave this tag unchanged.
