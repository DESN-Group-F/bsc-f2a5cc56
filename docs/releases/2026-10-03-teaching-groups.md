## Included behavior

The shared staff platform now includes manual scan selection in both inventories, grouped filters, whole-row battery selection, ordering, latest observed-location filtering and explicitly English date/time controls. Existing native staff authority, responsible owner versus current holder, exact return bindings and request recovery remain required.

Model-assisted registration uses conservative suggestions from the pinned reference catalog, consistent registered models or staff-created shared templates. Staff set common details for demonstration intake, stage editable scans and explicitly confirm registrations with automatic permanent IDs and server registration times. Staff may review and confirm optional-reason retirement/permanent removal batches after loans are returned. Identities and history remain retained.

Teaching groups is a primary sidebar workspace and an upper inventory-category shortcut. Groups are private to the native account and dataset; batteries and actual operations remain shared. Fixed group views support filtering, selection, downloads and reviewed group operations. Administrators may apply common owner/storage/charge/demo-observation updates. Scanning can add selected/completed assets to groups, and intake additions require confirmed registration. Back to previous page remembers the entry view and retains the originating battery list during a direct group visit.

Activity presents group operations as expandable entries containing original member evidence. Downloads offer group summaries or original member actions with selectable complete battery sections. Recorded group names, versions and actual affected members survive later definition edits and archiving. The existing Recurring tasks, personal Messages and native account workflow remain included.
