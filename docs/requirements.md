## Access interpretation

The platform serves staff. New checkout responsibility is the authenticated staff account, with a read-only holder display and no administrator proxy checkout. A login account is linked explicitly to its inventory people record using the stable account ID; identical display names do not link identities. Responsible asset ownership, the current holder and the staff member receiving a return remain separate.

Responsible owners are selected from active native staff accounts. The read-only staff directory projects account identities into each inventory; names are never used to link records. No student directory or legacy-borrower filter category is needed for this staff platform. The current development business data is disposable and was rebuilt locally rather than adding compatibility features for old test fixtures. Normal operations still retain their transaction history.

Staff can create a new battery but cannot edit its saved metadata. CSV import creates reviewed new records; it is not an overwrite shortcut. Administrators maintain buildings and rooms, charging records, demo observations and reasoned corrections. Staff identity and display names are maintained through accounts, not a separate editable people list. All staff can read/download business evidence. Account administration and its private audit are administrator-only; every account can manage its own profile.

All active staff may mark returned assets Scrapped or Permanently removed individually or in batches after reviewing exact versions/tags. The batch reason is optional; empty or whitespace-only reasons remain missing evidence and are displayed as Not recorded. An active loan must first be returned. Retirement does not delete the record, release its tag, change its owner or establish physical disposal. Both roles may explicitly recover Scrapped assets with their identity, original dates, specifications and history retained; no ordinary retired-metadata editing is added. Permanently removed assets remain terminal. Administrators may separately and irreversibly delete selected Scrapped battery records, histories and system-held related asset snapshots. Other assets, Task/group definitions and independent human notes remain; minimal deleted-ID references explain existing targets. BAT ID/deleting account/time evidence and technical tag-hash/request reservations prevent reuse and delayed writes. Unless explicitly deleted, retired records remain indefinitely. Default inventory views exclude retired assets. Record status filters expose all/scrapped/permanently removed records; In store and In use always refer to active assets. Personal archive downloads keep account-based responsibility bounds. Removal-history downloads capture the exact matching IDs and cannot expand to unrelated inventory; deleted data cannot be downloaded.

Recurring tasks are shared business records, visible and downloadable to all staff. Only administrators create/edit their configuration. A currently assigned staff member or administrator may confirm a reviewed open cycle's actual completion. Completion notes are optional, with no minimum length and a maximum of 2,000 characters; completion time and actor are always recorded. Server permissions and the submitted cycle version govern completion; read state, ownership and borrowing do not substitute for assignment. Each account's Messages is personal and can only be read, marked read, removed, restored or downloaded by that authenticated recipient. An administrator does not gain access to another account's inbox.

All staff share working business data across buildings and rooms. A filter narrows a view or download; it does not create a private inventory or a location-based access restriction. Demonstration and working data remain separate.

Teaching groups is a primary sidebar workspace over private saved definitions and shared battery records. Each account can create, edit and archive its own groups, including a name, optional notes and 1–100 distinct existing battery IDs. Groups persist on the server and remain available after signing in on another device; Working and Demonstration groups remain separate. Administrators also manage only their own groups. Archiving retains the private definition/history without removing batteries. Current use, responsible ownership and retirement do not silently change fixed membership. Cards open a group whose membership remains a fixed boundary through status, ordinary filters, selection and downloads.

The ordinary inventory/personal group criterion intersects membership with current filters. Select group batteries explicitly replaces the selection with available members within its fixed personal view, including retired records for review/download; excluded members are reported. Saving/selecting does not itself operate on assets. Within the primary group workspace, whole-group movements report ineligible members and require review/confirmation; selected operations retain existing permissions and exact asset/loan bindings. Administrators may apply a common owner, registered storage, charging record or demo observation atomically. All staff may move/retire eligible selected members. Only server-confirmed intake records are eligible for group membership. Scanning can explicitly choose group context before processing, or add selected/completed assets afterward without rewriting earlier history.

The server resolves the native account's active group and reviewed version, verifies each actual member and guards that definition at commit. Recorded business evidence snapshots the group name/version and actual affected battery IDs. Definition edits remain private; actual group inventory operations are shared and appear as expandable Activity entries. Renaming, changing membership or archiving cannot rewrite earlier operations. Group, movement, lifecycle and common-update requests retain exact recovery and native authorization guards after uncertain responses.

My activity filters inventory audit events by the immutable operator account ID resolved on the server. Responsible ownership and current borrowing do not determine which actions appear here. Renaming an account or sharing its display name with another account does not change historical attribution. Group entries retain that personal operator boundary. Search, historic-group filtering, pagination, selection and downloads cover all stored matching events beyond the snapshot's recent-history limit. Shared Activity history remains available to all staff. Private account-management audit and sign-in events are outside this inventory view.

Task creation provides a prominent Start from scratch entry for the Custom task category, alongside three optional prepared templates: storage-area review, inventory reconciliation and storage maintenance review. Templates reduce setup work without limiting creation to preset instructions. Their suggested schedules remain a provisional weekly area interval, explicit confirmed teaching-period dates and a provisional six-calendar-month interval for applicable assets. Custom tasks support all five existing target kinds: defined inventory, storage area, battery model, defined group and selected batteries. The blank custom form leaves title, instructions, applicable basis, scope, dates and reminder time for the administrator to specify. Storage review targets a storage area; maintenance targets a saved model, an explicitly selected group or selected batteries; reconciliation can also target a defined inventory. Initial area/reconciliation assignment uses the enabling administrator. A responsible-owner shortcut resolves existing active owner account IDs for selected assets without inferring names. Staff overrides remain explicit.

The editor exposes the task category, instructions, targets, assignees, schedule and final review. Changing the category preserves entered instructions and schedule; an incompatible target requires explicit correction before activation. Specific dates / one-off task supports one confirmed date for a single task or several explicit dates; no date is inferred. Saving a custom plan uses the same administrative authority, version checks, exact-request recovery and audit path as prepared plans. Migration 0016 adds the custom category without rewriting saved plans, cycle snapshots, assignments, removed-state evidence or messages. This is included in source release 0.7.0; source publication does not apply hosted migrations.

Activation requires the applicable basis, required work, confirmed scope, valid target, dates, explicit Sydney reminder time and active assignees, plus confirmation. Drafts can preserve missing information. Working storage-area activation rejects placeholder rooms; demonstration placeholders remain labelled provisional. Model/group membership is recorded when each cycle is created. Plans may be draft, active or paused; at most one open cycle exists per plan. Edits retain the open cycle's content/targets/due date and all history while updating reminder settings and current routing independently. Version conflicts preserve user input until explicit latest-state review.

Plan removal is a recoverable shared visibility change available only to administrators after review. It retains the original draft/active/paused configuration state, assignments, recurrence, open cycle, recorded requirements/due date and all completion evidence. A removed plan cannot be edited, generate new cycles/messages or have its outstanding cycle completed. Restore returns the same plan and existing cycle without resetting its deadline or inventing a completion; subsequent generation obeys the retained state and recurrence. All staff can inspect and download Current or Removed plan records. Removal/restoration uses the reviewed record version and an exact request ID; stale or uncertain outcomes cannot be silently treated as success.

Calendar recurrence retains its original anchor, including month-end behavior; six months is not 180 days. Completion-based recurrence uses the actual completion date. Explicit teaching-period dates are never inferred. After a late completion, calendar/explicit rules select a configured date after the reviewed due date and actual completion, with skipped planning periods recorded rather than invented completions. Reminder advance and time are separate from due date. Sydney ambiguous/nonexistent reminder times have no assumed UTC instant and are visibly unavailable.

Messages are evaluated during authenticated use when the configured reminder instant has been reached. One persistent message per cycle/recipient reminder is deduplicated; completed/paused tasks, removed plans and former assignees receive no new reminders. Reads preserve creation/read history and never complete a task. Existing messages retain the current linked task state. Downloads recommend Excel reports and also offer technical JSON. Both formats use the same plan visibility/search or own message visibility/task-state/read-state/search scope as the view and retain associated cycle history; they are separate from battery-detail exports. Excel resolves named staff and presents instructions, targets, schedules, status and completion evidence without replacing the retained technical source records. A red dot indicates any unread own message still in Inbox, independently of its task state or the current filters. Email preferences are saved, but email delivery and unattended scheduling are unavailable. No receipt, efficiency or safety-certification claim follows from this implementation.

Removing a message affects only its recipient's Inbox visibility. Removed keeps the same identity, content, created/read evidence and linked cycle, and Restore returns that record without marking it read. The shared task and other recipients' messages remain unchanged. Inbox/Removed is retained by Clear filters; task-state/read-state/search criteria reset independently. Eligible open tasks expose Complete task, completed cycles expose View completion, and unavailable tasks expose View task with the reason: a removed plan or lack of current assignment/administrator authority. The To do view excludes cycles belonging to removed plans. Task dialogs foreground the recorded deadline and assignments, keep detailed requirements initially collapsed and retain visible footer actions outside the scrolling body. A confirmation is required before recording actual completion; neither reading nor removing a message supplies it.

Detailed downloads select information sections rather than individual scalar fields. Excel and JSON retain multiple related tables; CSV is available for flat summaries and directory/activity lists. Each generated file carries the downloaded scope and counts. Data can change between the displayed view and generation; the file is internally consistent at its read boundary.

Excel reports for inventory, complete battery details, activity, directories, tasks and Messages begin with Report guide. It explains report creation, inventory, filters, scope, counts and the purpose of each sheet; it is descriptive information, not a form to complete. Business worksheets use readable headings, explicit units, named actors, retained historic labels and typed dates/times labelled for Australia/Sydney. Unknown values remain explicit rather than becoming zero or inferred evidence. Original fields, internal identifiers, UTC timestamps and JSON remain in hidden Evidence worksheets. The guide explains Excel's Unhide action, including access to Complete text parts for values exceeding one cell. JSON retains structured technical data. Human reports do not drop complete history or widen the selected record scope. Shared directory download controls use Excel; Owner ID for imports and Room ID for imports preserve stable references needed by the existing import workflow.

Staff accounts provides an administrator-only Download Excel report action using the same guide/business/evidence layout. It exports only the allowed account identity, profile, role, status, preference and creation/update fields, never passwords, hashes, sessions or authorization versions. The existing administrator-only CSV endpoint remains available with readable headings and the same scope. Neither format includes account-management audit events or exposes another account's Messages.

Activity downloads offer selected operations or all matching operations, with group summaries in Excel/CSV/JSON or original member evidence plus selectable full battery sections in Excel/JSON. A summary group row contains the historic group name and count without member detail expansion; ordinary individual operations retain their own records. Detailed Activity members are original operation snapshots, while selected battery tables describe export-time records and complete stored histories. Member search retains the whole group operation. Selected IDs ignore ordinary criteria but cannot widen the authenticated all/mine scope; unavailable selections reject the whole request.

Row checkboxes support an explicit selected-battery download. A nonempty selection becomes the default scope in the download dialog and is preserved when switching between summary and detailed information. Selected IDs are independent of ordinary filter criteria and page, so a selection can span pages and filters. The fixed personal view still applies: if an asset is reassigned or returned and leaves that view, the selection requires explicit review and the server rejects the whole download with HTTP 409. The server resolves the personal account from authentication, never client identity fields. Unavailable or foreign-inventory IDs are also rejected rather than silently omitted. Download metadata records the deduplicated selected IDs, count and filter context. Requests that exceed the existing 15,000-byte JSON limit show an explicit error; the selection is not truncated or replaced with the whole inventory.

One Filter panel contains search, chemistry/model, building/room, responsible owner/current holder, capacity/voltage, manufacture age and latest checkout/charge dates, plus collapsed Teaching groups and Sort order sections. Status remains in the All batteries / In store / In use tabs. Applied filters lists nondefault criteria above results with individual removal and Clear filters. Clearing restores default Battery ID ascending order and removes an optional group criterion, while preserving selections and fixed My batteries / My batteries in use scope. Inside an opened Teaching group, its group membership stays fixed and the redundant group criterion is hidden. If previously selected members leave that group, explicit review/removal is required before an operation or selected download. Chemistry is the saved material classification, not an inferred use purpose. Unknown specifications and dates have explicit choices. Date ranges use Sydney calendar dates; age uses the same captured calculation date as the export. Counts, pages and filtered summary/detail downloads share the same query engine.

Sort order supports Battery ID, name, registration time, manufacture date, manufacture age, time in service, latest checkout/charge/observation time, capacity, voltage and latest charging duration. Ascending puts smaller numbers or earlier dates first; descending reverses known values. Ages compare elapsed days, so descending age means older assets first. Unknown values stay last in either direction. Equal values use natural Battery ID ascending order with an exact-string fallback. Sort the complete matching set before slicing pages, and retain the same order in summary/detail Batteries tables for filtered, page and selected downloads. Export metadata records ordering and the applied private group; complete historical evidence remains intact.

The sidebar and page shortcuts open the persistent Scan checkout / Scan return station. The left panel accepts registered tag identifiers; the right Manual selection panel offers eligible batteries in both inventories, including batteries without tags. Search and Clear filters preserve selections. Tag entry and Add selected to queue stage batteries in the Review queue without submitting a movement. Staff review 1–100 batteries, remove unwanted entries and explicitly choose Confirm checkout or Confirm return. There is no mode selector or immediate-save path. Repeated tag entry can populate the list; one input source is retained per queue. Successful processing never exits the station and clears only verified completed choices. Repeated tags, unknown tags and ineligible states receive explicit feedback. Input and reviewed source/room settings are frozen during a write. An interrupted write is retried with the same request ID and captured payload before its result can be treated as resolved. Unfinished queues saved by older Continuous sessions recover into the review workflow without automatic submission; uncertain attempts retain their immutable requests, and retry never commits remaining queued entries. Manual selection is a distinct saved source; previous simulated evidence remains unchanged. No connected-reader claim follows from either input method. First-time battery registration remains a separate draft-and-confirm intake workflow, not a return.

Existing selected-row movement dialogs remain available and show the selected list first. Adding existing registered batteries is optional and supports search/manual tag lookup. Return selected retains the explicitly chosen list, including other staff loans. A stale return cannot close a subsequent loan, and a stale correction cannot change its intended action; the original review remains available for explicit reconsideration. Scan transactions additionally guard the reviewed battery/tag/version bindings. Optional return room confirmation records time, source and label snapshots in the same atomic transaction; it neither rewrites registered home nor establishes hardware location. Unverified live room placement is unavailable.

## New arrivals

New arrivals is a region within shared Battery inventory and a removable inventory criterion, not a fourth status tab. Personal inventory views and opened Teaching groups do not offer this entry. Opening the region clears ordinary search/filter criteria, selects All batteries and active records, and orders registration time newest first while retaining chosen batteries. This prevents a previous search or group criterion from hiding the arrivals acknowledged by that click. Staff may then apply additional filters to the recent view. All batteries / In store / In use retain their recorded-loan meanings.

The region narrows existing rows to active assets first registered less than 48 elapsed hours before the server reporting time. Those rows show Newly registered, including assets registered by the viewer. This indicates recent entry into the register; it does not establish a newly manufactured, unused or physically healthy battery. Counts, pagination and filtered downloads compose the same recent-registration criterion with later ordinary criteria. Ordinary filters do not acknowledge arrivals or clear the account's global sidebar alert.

Red dots on Battery inventory and its inventory categories identify unread arrivals registered by another native account in the selected dataset. Identical display names do not merge actors, and the responsible owner does not determine who registered an asset. Read state is separate for each native account and for Working and Demonstration inventory, and is shared across that account's sessions. Clicking New arrivals deliberately acknowledges the registrations through the cursor captured from the displayed snapshot. Navigation to the inventory, rendering rows, polling and refreshing do not acknowledge them, even if the staff member is already viewing the list when another account registers a battery. An arrival committed after the captured cursor stays unread. An older tab or retry cannot move acknowledgement backward. If an acknowledgement or its refresh fails, the explicit retry keeps its original captured cursor rather than acknowledging subsequent arrivals. Reading does not remove a Newly registered label early.

The label, recent count and unread dot disappear at 48 hours. Scrapped and Permanently removed assets are excluded immediately; deleted assets are unavailable. Returns, metadata edits and recovery do not create a first-registration event or reset its time. A recovered asset can qualify only for the remainder of its original registration window, with its earlier reading state preserved.

Formal manual registration, reviewed CSV creation, confirmed demo intake and confirmed operator-entered mobile registration supply native first-registration evidence. The existing mobile registration source can affect the shared register without changing the mobile prototype's release boundary. Staged drafts, client scan times and seeded demo initialization do not produce arrivals. Historical backfill uses attributable native registration evidence only; unknown actors are not guessed. Existing accounts start with historical registrations acknowledged when this feature is introduced. A newly created account starts at each inventory's current cursor and receives alerts for subsequent registrations, while recent labels remain visible for earlier qualifying stock.

Visible idle inventory refresh runs every ten seconds and on window focus, pausing around inventory review dialogs and persistent workflows. The browser advances the last server reporting time using elapsed monotonic time so labels can expire between polls without trusting the device's calendar clock. This is eventual refresh, not a push notification or background delivery guarantee. No release is published or deployed by this local addition.

## Interpretation boundaries

Last observed location is derived from the selected battery's latest stored observation, ordered by observation time and receipt time, with a deterministic stored-row tie break. It is separate from registered storage and does not establish present physical location. Room filtering uses the observation's existing room identity; its address/time/source remain recorded evidence. Dropdown labels use saved observation labels, with room IDs only where needed to distinguish identical names or unavailable labels. A directory rename cannot backfill a historic address. The Not observed option has no reading; Recorded address unavailable has a reading without saved address labels. Clearing this criterion does not clear selection, personal scope or other criteria.

Persistent station exit controls remain visible while scrolling. Exiting does not bypass uncertain-result handling or storage recovery. Scanning keeps its existing reviewed-exit confirmation; intake retains staged entries and captured requests in tab storage. Retirement/removal can return to the inventory only when the current confirmation/storage state permits navigation, retaining its review draft. Application date/time controls use English independently of the browser's native date UI, retain ISO strings and 24-hour values, and do not fill omitted dates or time parts. Existing server validation remains authoritative, including Sydney daylight-saving ambiguity checks.

**In store** means an active asset has no active loan recorded; it does not prove physical presence. **In use** means an active asset has a recorded checkout to a staff holder; it does not establish physical use, current draw or reader detection. Terminal assets are outside both counts. **Storage room** is the registered home. **Last observed room** is time/source evidence. Missing detection is not proof of loss.

The earlier student-operated scan idea was superseded by staff confirmation. A photo cannot read an RFID chip. Reader transport and room performance remain unspecified; manual identifiers and record selection support software review.

The makerspace list is a reference, not a verified university storage directory. Both inventories contain **J18 - Willis Annexe**, **E10 - Hilmer Building** and **G17 - Electrical Engineering Building**, supported by [UNSW's makerspace directory](https://www.making.unsw.edu.au/makerspaces/about/). Only J18 is selectable; other building names are greyed out and their rooms are Not available. J18 contains Demo room and Demo workspace with explicit Placeholder flags. Actual room numbers and storage names await confirmation. Renaming a room does not automatically verify it. Working inventory has no seeded batteries; real battery details remain pending.

Existing versioned migrations and database evidence guards remain intact. This does not require retaining disposable old preview fixtures or adding UI compatibility categories for them. Unknown specifications, ages and historic charging durations are not inferred.

Battery age uses manufacture and first-use dates, stored as calendar dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Age since manufacture and time in service are calculated as of the current Sydney date. Confirmed demo intake defaults first use to the server registration date, as explicitly agreed by the user; this is an operational convention, not proof of earlier use. Staff may instead record an actual earlier first-use date or leave service start unknown. Manufacture date is optional and is never inferred from registration. Future dates and first use before manufacture are rejected. Administrators can correct saved dates through the existing versioned edit and audit process. Single registration and CSV imports continue to accept explicit optional `manufactured_on` and `first_used_on` values. Registration time is stored separately and unchanged by loans or metadata edits. Summary downloads and selected detailed specifications include registration time, the lifecycle dates, calculated age in days and the calculation date.
